LearnKeyboard Privacy Policy

App: LearnKeyboard: Piano Lessons (com.finessedev.learnkeyboard) · Developer: GeeksCraft (FINESSE DEV LTD) · Effective: 18 November 2026

LearnKeyboard is a piano and learning app. It is built to run on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we (the developer) do not run a server that receives your recordings or playing.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your settings, progress, badges, recordings, imported songs and exported files are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Microphone: if you use "listen to my piano" or "song from audio", the sound is analysed on your device to detect notes. The audio is not uploaded and is not kept unless you choose to save a recording.
  • Song from audio uses an on-device model (Basic Pitch, Apache-2.0). Nothing is sent to a server for it.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

2. Data that leaves your device

  • Optional sound pack download: if you choose the Concert Grand HD piano, the app downloads sample files from a public file host (tonejs.github.io). That host can see your IP address and the request, as with any download.
  • Wi-Fi duet: the duet feature talks directly to another phone on your local network. It does not go through our servers. Local network access is used only for this.
  • Sharing and exporting: when you share a recording, MIDI or video, it goes to the app you pick (for example chat or email). That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent first, and on iOS it asks permission before tracking. You can change your choice later in Settings or in your phone's privacy settings.
  • Purchases: the one-time Pro purchase (removes ads) is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive only confirmation that it is valid, not your payment details.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: to hear your real piano and for song from audio.
  • Local network and Wi-Fi: for the Wi-Fi duet.
  • Bluetooth / USB: to connect a MIDI keyboard.
  • Notifications: for optional practice reminders, scheduled on your device.

4. Children

LearnKeyboard is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the app's Settings, or remove ads with the Pro purchase.
  • Delete any recording in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data.
  • Because we hold no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Contact us with any question.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
